Help & User Guide

Step-by-step instructions for new front-desk staff.

1

Signing in

Use the username and password issued by the clinic administrator on the login screen. Only authorized staff accounts can access the system.

2

Registering a new appointment

From the Main Menu choose Register New Appointment. Fill in the patient's name, address and contact number, then pick a dentist, treatment type, date and time. The system automatically rejects a time slot if the chosen dentist is already booked, and prevents booking a date/time in the past.

3

Finding an appointment

Choose Display Appointment Details and enter the appointment number shown to the patient at booking (format APT-000001). All stored details are displayed on one screen.

4

Calculating and printing a bill

Open the appointment, then click Calculate & Print Bill. The system prices the visit automatically (returning patients with 3 or more completed visits receive a loyalty discount) and shows a printable receipt.

5

Reports (administrators)

Administrators can open Reports to view Daily Appointments, Revenue, or Dentist Workload for any date range - useful for end-of-day reconciliation and planning.

6

Exiting the system

Always choose Logout from the menu when you are finished, especially on a shared front-desk computer, so the session cannot be used by anyone else.
